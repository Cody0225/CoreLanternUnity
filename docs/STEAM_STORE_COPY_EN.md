## 4. Long Description (Steam page body)

```
═══════════════════════════════════════
■ Defend the Core. 10 Waves of Defense.
═══════════════════════════════════════

"Eggcore Protocol" is a defense × roguelite × survivor fusion where you protect 
a central core called the "Data Egg" while pushing back endless waves of enemies.

You play as an autonomous-firing companion, moving around the core to collect 
data, acquire modules, and evolve through 10 waves of escalating threat.

Each run takes about 15-20 minutes. Easy to start, deep to lose yourself in.

═══════════════════════════════════════
■ Key Features
═══════════════════════════════════════

◆ 11 Unique Partner Characters
   From the balanced Cobalt Pup to the core-resonant Solar Anchor.
   Each has signature mechanics (funnel bits / laser / aura / etc.).

◆ 3 Evolution Routes + 6 Cross-Evolutions
   SPEED / POWER / GUARD across 4 stages (L0→L3).
   Meet specific conditions to unlock 6 cross-evolutions (Nova Aegis, etc.).

◆ 5 Stages × Unique Enemies × Unique Hazards
   • Stage 1: Lantern Field — Standard defense
   • Stage 2: Lava Cache — Lava pools + heat vents
   • Stage 3: Broken Core Network — Corruption + relay rewards
   • Stage 4: Frost Vault — Frost crystals + slow patches
   • Stage 5: Storm Spire — Random lightning strikes

◆ Boss Phase 2 + Mid-Boss Enrage
   Wave 5 Pulswyrm: enrages at 50% HP, intensifying bullet patterns + charging.
   Wave 10 Nullwyrm: enters Phase 2 at 50% HP.
   New attack patterns unlock: Spiral Corruption / Core Mark / Glitch Summon.

◆ Player-Friendly Choices
   • Reroll / Lock / Banish: Brotato-style card management
   • Same-settings rematch: One button to retry with the same setup
   • Danger Level 0-5: Choose your own difficulty
   • Accessibility modifiers

◆ 60+ Modules + 15+ Relics
   Not just POWER builds — SPEED and GUARD specialized builds are equally strong.
   Character-specific signature modules and cross-evolution exclusive modules.

═══════════════════════════════════════
■ About Early Access
═══════════════════════════════════════

This game is currently in Early Access.
• 11 partners / 5 stages / 10 waves / all boss fights are fully playable
```
