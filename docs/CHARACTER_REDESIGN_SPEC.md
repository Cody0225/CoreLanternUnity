# CoreLanternUnity Character Redesign Spec Draft

Last updated: 2026-05-22

This is a draft only. Final character images remain a later task and should be approved by the user before large-scale generation.

## Design Goal

Create a commercial-safe original partner creature lineup that keeps the appeal of:

- cute base partner
- visible evolution
- build identity through shape
- cross evolution where the main partner absorbs a link

But avoid direct copying of existing monster IP.

## Global Art Direction

Camera and readability:

- Top-down or 3/4 top-down.
- Readable at `48px`, attractive at `96px`, source at `512x512`.
- Strong silhouette first, texture second.
- Transparent PNG for character assets.

Material language:

- Dark cyber organism + clean neon accents.
- Soft creature body at base level.
- Increasing armor, light channels, and signature appendages by evolution level.
- Avoid over-detailed sprite noise.

Evolution growth rule:

- L0: mascot-simple, one strong motif.
- L1: first route equipment appears.
- L2: body proportion changes and combat role becomes obvious.
- L3: hero form, 30-45% larger on screen, clear signature silhouette.

Route shape language:

- SPEED: slim body, boosters, blade fins, motion trails, cyan/blue.
- POWER: wide shoulders, horns, cannons, furnace cores, orange/gold.
- GUARD: rounded armor, shields, rings, shell plates, green/mint.

## Base Partner Silhouette Concepts

Requested baseline is 8 existing base partners plus `Halo Caster` as the 9th partner. If concurrent Claude work keeps `Pulse Hydra` in the roster, treat it as a provisional 10th partner that needs the same visual standard before commercial-facing use.

### 1. Cobalt Pup

Role:

- Standard balanced starter.

Silhouette:

- Small cyber wolf/pup with large triangular ears.
- Short body, confident stance, scarf-like data tail.
- Face has one bright visor mark, not a helmet clone.

Evolution direction:

- SPEED: becomes a sleek rail-wolf with leg boosters.
- POWER: gains shoulder launchers and heavier jaw armor.
- GUARD: gains circular shield collar and plated forelegs.

### 2. Ember Drake

Role:

- Projectile and pressure starter.

Silhouette:

- Small orange dragon-lizard.
- Furnace belly glow.
- Stub horns and short tail.
- No large wings at base, to keep it distinct from bird silhouettes.

Evolution direction:

- SPEED: jet-lizard with ember trails.
- POWER: heavy drake with twin cannon horns.
- GUARD: plated salamander with heat shield scales.

### 3. Sage Hare

Role:

- Defensive and recovery starter.

Silhouette:

- Green jackrabbit with long antenna-like ears.
- Paw pads read like shield glyphs.
- Small round satchel/core charm on chest.

Evolution direction:

- SPEED: long-eared relay runner.
- POWER: spring-legged striker with impact gauntlets.
- GUARD: shrine-guardian hare with shield ears.

### 4. Hex Cat

Role:

- Chain and residual-effect starter.

Silhouette:

- Purple cat with angular ears and hex mask.
- Thin chain-rune tail.
- Small floating hex shard near shoulder.

Evolution direction:

- SPEED: phase-cat with split afterimage tail.
- POWER: curse artillery cat with floating hex cannons.
- GUARD: ward-cat with orbiting hex barrier.

### 5. Drift Fox

Role:

- Evasion and phase movement starter.

Silhouette:

- White/pink fox, unmistakably mammal.
- Long brush tail, pointed muzzle, fox ears.
- No beak, no crest, no feathers, no bird feet.
- Phase ribbon wraps around body.

Evolution direction:

- SPEED: long-tailed comet fox.
- POWER: fox with shard lances and heavier forelimbs.
- GUARD: fox with mirror-shield tails.

### 6. Iron Bear

Role:

- Heavy armor starter.

Silhouette:

- Black/gold bear cub with rounded mass.
- Piston-like forearms.
- Armored brow and short ears.
- Slow but sturdy stance.

Evolution direction:

- SPEED: compact armored roller-bear with wheel-like pads.
- POWER: huge piledriver bear.
- GUARD: fortress bear with back plating and shield claws.

### 7. Wraith Lynx

Role:

- Melee, lifesteal, high-risk close combat.

Silhouette:

- Silver/white lynx with low stalking pose.
- Long ear tufts.
- Spectral claws and torn data cloak.
- Not too wolf-like; emphasize feline shoulders and tail.

Evolution direction:

- SPEED: blink assassin lynx.
- POWER: claw breaker with oversized spectral paws.
- GUARD: ghost sentinel with rib-like armor.

### 8. Genesis Core

Role:

- Secret all-link or high-progression partner.

Silhouette:

- Floating golden/cyan core familiar.
- Egg-like jewel body with small modular wings/fins.
- More abstract than the animal partners.

Evolution direction:

- SPEED: orbital comet core.
- POWER: reactor core with cannon petals.
- GUARD: cathedral shield core with halo plates.

### 9. Halo Caster

Role:

- Funnel/bit partner.

Silhouette:

- Small caster creature with a central mascot body and three satellite bits.
- Bits form a halo triangle around it.
- Body should not look like a bird; avoid wings, beak, and feather crests.

Evolution direction:

- SPEED: bits become fast blade drones.
- POWER: bits become charge cannons.
- GUARD: bits become shield pylons.

### 10. Pulse Hydra (Provisional)

Role:

- Sustained beam / multi-head laser partner.

Silhouette:

- Compact hydra-drone with three short neck emitters.
- Central small creature body, not a large dragon boss.
- Beam heads should read as organic-tech serpentine nodes, not generic turrets.

Evolution direction:

- SPEED: three thin beam heads with quick sweeping trails.
- POWER: heavier emitter heads and reactor chest.
- GUARD: heads become shield-beam anchors around the body.

Production note:

- This partner was not part of the original 9-body request, so final inclusion should be confirmed by the user.

## Cross Evolution Concepts

Cross evolution should show the main character absorbing link power. The base species must remain recognizable.

### Nova Aegis

Links:

- Nova + Bulwark

Look:

- Shoulder cannons plus shield crown.
- Cyan attack core embedded inside green guard frame.
- The partner gains a forward-facing heroic stance.

Gameplay read:

- Pursuit damage and defense both active.

### Photon Siphon

Links:

- Nova + Siphon

Look:

- Solar intake rings around chest or tail.
- Vacuum-like data veins pulling particles inward.
- Bright cyan/gold center with dark outer rim.

Gameplay read:

- Aggressive collection and ranged pressure.

### Core Bastion

Links:

- Bulwark + Siphon

Look:

- Fortress armor, lock-shaped chest core, broad guard silhouette.
- Heaviest cross form.

Gameplay read:

- Core defense and sustain.

### Nova Phantom

Links:

- Nova + Phase

Look:

- Split afterimage cloak.
- Bright blue ghost cannons or blade trails.
- Body remains sleek and readable.

Gameplay read:

- Burst damage with evasive repositioning.

### Aegis Drift

Links:

- Bulwark + Phase

Look:

- Shield skates, mirror plates, translucent phase barrier.
- Defensive but mobile.

Gameplay read:

- Avoidance plus protection.

### Photon Wraith

Links:

- Siphon + Phase

Look:

- Dark mint siphon halo, spectral claws, hollow glow channels.
- More eerie than heroic, but still original and readable.

Gameplay read:

- Collection, lifesteal, and evasive offense.

## Commercial-Safe Differentiation

From monster-evolution franchises:

- Use protocol/module language and cyber guardian motifs, not creature taxonomy.
- Avoid familiar monster silhouettes from well-known IP.
- Avoid names ending or structured like existing franchise naming conventions.
- Evolution is "protocol morphing" driven by route modules and link absorption.

From `Cult of the Lamb`:

- Avoid occult cult pageantry, ritual robes, and sermon framing.
- Keep the identity as cyber core guardians and data survival.

From `Slay the Spire`:

- Cards are build choices, not the main world fiction.
- Persistent partner evolution is the emotional anchor.

From `Vampire Survivors`:

- Keep readable projectile chaos, but differentiate through core defense, partner growth, and cross evolution.

## Asset Production Rules

Final generation should use:

- `512x512` source PNG.
- Transparent background.
- No text inside character images.
- No logos or symbols resembling known brands.
- Single centered creature.
- Stable camera angle across all partners.
- Enough padding for glow and evolution cut-ins.

Runtime sizing:

- L0 display: base scale.
- L1 display: `1.10x`
- L2 display: `1.22x`
- L3 display: `1.35x`
- Cross evolution display: `1.38x` unless it harms gameplay readability.
